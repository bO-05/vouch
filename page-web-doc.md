- **Tags**: `#devchallenge`, `#weekendchallenge`, `#hackathon`

---

## 💡 The Prompt

> **Build something in the spirit of generosity.**

Interpret this prompt however you'd like. Examples:
- A personal donation tracker
- A volunteer matching tool
- Something that makes a small nonprofit's day-to-day less painful
- A project that raises awareness for a cause you care about
- Any angle is fair game.

### Jumping-off Themes (UN Charity Themes)
* **Technology-driven giving**: Crowdfunding, mobile donations, tools making giving accessible and transparent.
* **Youth leadership**: Social ventures and community projects led by people closest to the problem.
* **Equity and inclusion**: Solutions accounting for how race, gender, disability, and geography shape need.
* **Ethical and accountable giving**: Transparency, clear reporting, community-driven design.
* **Climate and poverty**: Resilience tools for communities hit first and hardest.

## 🏆 Prize Categories

Five winners total ($200 USD + DEV++ Membership + exclusive badge each):
1. **Overall Winner** (does not require any partner tech)
2. **Best use of [Snowflake](https://www.snowflake.com/en/)**
3. **Best use of [Solana](https://solana.com/)**
4. **Best use of [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)**
5. **Best use of [Google AI](https://aistudio.google.com/)**

> **Note**: You may use multiple technologies in a single submission to qualify for multiple categories, but participants are limited to one win per challenge.

---

## ⚖️ Judging Criteria

1. **Relevance to Theme**: Does the project clearly connect to the spirit of the challenge?
2. **Creativity**: Is the concept original or an interesting take on a familiar problem?
3. **Technical Execution**: Is the project functional, well-built, and easy to use?
4. **Writing Quality**: Is the submission post clearly written and engaging?
5. **Use of Prize Category Technology** *(optional)*: If pursuing a prize category, does the submission meaningfully incorporate the chosen technology?

## 📝 Submission Structure / Template

To qualify, the project submission post requires:

- **What I Built**: What does it do and what was the intended goal?
- **Demo**: Embedded project, deployed link, or video demo.
- **Code**: GitHub repo link.
- **How I Built It**: Technical approach, architecture, decisions, and how sponsor technology was incorporated.
- **Prize Categories**: Identify target prize categories (e.g., Best Use of Google AI, ElevenLabs, Solana, Snowflake).
